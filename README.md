### CHEM-000489: Fluoride tunneling between two germanium centers of a molecular receptor
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Realize the proposed double-germanium fluoride receptor and detect low-temperature intersite transfer with a tunneling signature. Distinguish exchange from static disorder and thermal hopping.
- **Status:** The ping-pong process was predicted in 2018. The 2025 experimental fluorine-tunneling paper still describes this receptor as hypothetical; its observed polyfluoride system is chemically distinct.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Tests deliberate geometric control of tunneling by a relatively heavy nucleus.
- **Key works:** A. Nandi, A. Sucher, and S. Kozuch (2018), “Ping-Pong Tunneling Reactions: Can Fluoride Jump at Absolute Zero?” [DOI: 10.1002/chem.201802782](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201802782); C. Müller et al. (2025), “Experimental observation of quantum mechanical fluorine tunnelling,” [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-59008-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59008-6).
- **Related:** Quantum control—engineering tunneling barriers.
- **Aliases:** Fluoride ping-pong tunneling.
